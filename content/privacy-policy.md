This Privacy Policy describes how PromoteLiberty.org ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit promoteliberty.org (the "Site").

## Information We Collect

PromoteLiberty.org is a static website. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details unless you voluntarily provide it to us (for example, by emailing us).

## Cookies and Third-Party Embeds

Some pages on this Site embed third-party services that may set cookies or collect data on their own behalf, independent of us. Currently this includes:

- **Facebook Comments Plugin** — Meta Platforms, Inc. may collect data (such as IP address and browser information) when the comments widget loads or when you interact with it, in accordance with [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

We do not control the data collection practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

## Log Data

Our hosting provider may automatically collect standard web server log information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and operational purposes.

## Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
